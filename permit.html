Can My Offset count how much you use these?

It opens your own account on each one and adds things up for the last 30 days: how many prompts, videos, orders or rides. Untick any you would rather leave out.

Chrome will ask you once more to confirm. Only the totals leave each page. Your chats, what you watched, what you bought and where you went stay where they are, and so do your passwords. You can take this back whenever you like in Chrome's extension settings.
